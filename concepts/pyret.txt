```Example from the web:
data BinTree:
 | leaf
 | node(value, left, right)
end
fun tree-sum(t):
  doc: &quot;Calculate the sum of node values&quot;
  cases (BinTree) t:
    | leaf =&gt; 0
    | node(v, l, r) =&gt;
      v + tree-sum(l) + tree-sum(r)
  end
where:
  tree-sum(leaf) is 0
  node4 = node(4, leaf, leaf)
  tree-sum(node(5, node4, leaf)) is 9
end
```
