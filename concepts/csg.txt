The CSG file format is a text-based format used by OpenSCAD to represent constructive solid geometry (CSG) operations for 3D modeling. It defines a tree of geometric primitives and operations (union, difference, intersection) to create complex 3D models. CSG files are typically used as an intermediate representation in OpenSCAD for rendering and exporting 3D designs.
