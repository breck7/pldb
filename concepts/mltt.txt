Martin-Löf Type Theory (MLTT) is a formal system developed by Per Martin-Löf for constructive mathematics, combining typed lambda calculus with dependent types and the Curry-Howard correspondence. It serves as a foundation for proof assistants and functional programming languages, emphasizing constructive proofs as programs.
