```Example from <a href='https://github.com/marko-js/marko-tmbundle'>Linguist</a>:
$ var name = &#39;Frank&#39;;
$ var colors = [&#39;red&#39;, &#39;green&#39;, &#39;blue&#39;];

&lt;h1&gt;
    Hello ${name}!
&lt;/h1&gt;

&lt;ul if(colors.length)&gt;
    &lt;li style={color: color} for(color in colors)&gt;
        ${color}
    &lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;div else&gt;
    No colors!
&lt;/div&gt;
```
