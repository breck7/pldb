PENG
====

PENG, aka Processed English, is a <a href="../lists/explorer.html#searchBuilder=%7B%22criteria%22%3A%5B%7B%22condition%22%3A%22contains%22%2C%22data%22%3A%22tags%22%2C%22origData%22%3A%22tags%22%2C%22type%22%3A%22string%22%2C%22value%22%3A%5B%22knowledgeBase%22%5D%7D%5D%2C%22logic%22%3A%22AND%22%7D">knowledge base</a> created in 2002 by Rolf Schwitter and Marc Tilbrook.

 https://pldb.io/concepts/../lists/creators.html#q=Marc%20Tilbrook Marc Tilbrook

#2511 on PLDB
24 Years Old

 A controlled language processor translates PENG text to first-order logic via a discourse representation structure. The resultant logical theory can then be checked for consistency and informativity, and may also be used for question-answering by third-party reasoning services.
