USSR (Unified Shell Script REPL) is a general-purpose, multi-paradigm interpreted shell scripting language featuring a unique grammar resembling TCL and Conventional Commits. It is designed to work efficiently with low-quality code en masse and was partially AI-designed using ChatGPT.
